Expanding access to aviation education.

Future of Flight Equity works to identify barriers to aviation education and connect students and educators with opportunities across the aerospace industry.

Explore Our Research

Our Mission

We believe that a student's location, school funding, or background should not determine whether they can explore a future in aviation. Our work begins by researching educational gaps and creating accessible resources for schools and students.

Research & Insights

Our research examines access to aviation education in Wisconsin public high schools, including the funding, staffing, and resource barriers that prevent schools from developing programs.

Explore Opportunities

Discover aviation programs, scholarships, camps, internships, competitions, and other opportunities designed to help students explore aerospace careers.

Get Involved

Future of Flight Equity welcomes educators, students, aviation professionals, and community organizations interested in improving access to aviation education.
