Our mission

A student's ZIP code should not determine their future.

Aviation and aerospace offer pathways into engineering, technology, transportation, research, public service, and skilled careers. However, many students never have the chance to explore these fields through their schools.

Future of Flight Equity works to identify those gaps, share research, and make aviation opportunities easier for students and educators to find.

How we create impact

📊

🎓

🤝

Why it matters

Our research shows a clear access gap.

Future of Flight Equity surveyed Wisconsin schools to better understand the availability of aviation education and the barriers schools face.

25

Wisconsin schools contributed responses to our aviation education survey.

80%

Reported having no formal aviation education program.

76%

Identified funding as a major obstacle to program development.
